15,000

How the estimate works. Each contest reports its own registration and turnout, so ballots already counted in that jurisdiction = registration × reported turnout. The outstanding ballots you set above are spread across jurisdictions in proportion to the ballots each has already counted. For the top candidate not currently winning a seat, we show the votes needed to pass the last candidate in, and what share of that jurisdiction’s outstanding ballots it would take — assuming those ballots split only between those two candidates. It is a scenario, not a forecast of how anyone will vote.

Caveats. Results are unofficial until certified by the Monterey County Registrar of Voters. Outstanding ballots are not evenly spread, and not every ballot carries a vote in every contest. Some measures need a 55% or two-thirds majority to pass — this page shows which side leads, not whether a measure clears its threshold.
